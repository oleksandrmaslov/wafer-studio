Wafer Studio

A keymap editor for keyboards running ZMK Studio-enabled firmware. Connect over USB or Bluetooth, edit your layers, and save straight to the keyboard — no firmware rebuild and nothing to install.

Wafer Studio runs in the browser. A desktop build is available for Windows, macOS and Linux, and adds Bluetooth support on the platforms where browsers do not expose it.

Wafer Studio is an independent redesign based on ZMK Studio and is not an official ZMK project.
